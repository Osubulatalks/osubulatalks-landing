Four roles, each with its own ledger

Everyone signs into the same system, but nobody sees more than their job requires.

Admin
Full control. Sets up classes, terms, fee amounts, and the grading scale. Creates every staff login and manages the school's mobile-money settlement account.
Accountant
Records payments and tracks who owes what. Sends fee reminders to one family, or to every family with an overdue balance, in a single action.
Teacher
Enters exam scores for their own class, and sees fee status only where it's relevant, and nothing about other classes or the school's finances.
Parent
Sees their child's fee balance and results, downloads the report card, and pays outstanding fees by mobile money from their own phone.

What it does

No add-ons to unlock later. This is what runs today.

Students, classes, and terms
Holds your school's real structure; classes, academic terms, every enrolled student. Add one at a time, or bulk-import a whole class from a spreadsheet.
One system for Nursery, Primary, and Secondary together
A school running more than one level gets every class set up in one pass at onboarding, and each level keeps its own correct wording; pupils here, students there, throughout.
Boarding and day students, tracked separately
Mark each student Boarding or Day at enrolment, and the school's snapshot breaks the numbers down accordingly, no more counting by hand at the end of term.
Fee structures that bill themselves
Set tuition and optional items like transport or meals once, for a class and a term. Every student in that class gets a correct balance immediately, no manual bill per child.
Payments, cash, bank, or mobile money
An accountant records a cash or bank payment in seconds and the balance updates instantly. Parents can also pay by MTN or Airtel mobile money straight from their phone, no accountant involved.
Fee reminders by SMS
Send one family a reminder, or every overdue family across a class or the whole school, in a single action. Each message carries that child's own name and balance, never a generic broadcast.
Daily attendance, class by class
A teacher marks the register in minutes each morning. The record stays with the student, searchable by class, term, or a single child, instead of living in a book that never leaves the classroom.
Exams, grading, and report cards
Enter scores on a simple grid. The system grades against your school's own scale and ranks each student in their class automatically. One click produces a report card with results and fee balance together.
Grading built for the Ugandan curriculum
Compulsory subjects, weighted CA and End-of-Cycle scoring, and certification eligibility follow UNEB's own rules, not a generic scale bent to fit. Set it up once per school and every report card follows it automatically.
One final result from several exams
Combine a mock, a midterm, and a final into a single composite result with its own weighting, the way a real end-of-cycle certificate is built, not just an average of scores.
Student ID cards, printed on demand
Upload a photo once, and print a ready-to-laminate ID card whenever the school needs one. No design software, no third-party printer.
Works even when the internet doesn't
Recording a payment, entering scores, adding a student; all of it keeps working with no connection at all. Everything saves on the device and syncs the moment signal returns. Nothing lost, nothing recorded twice.
Built for one school, or many
Every school's data stays completely separate and private. A school never sees, and can never reach, another school's students, fees, or results.
Installable like an app
Works in any browser, and adds to a phone's home screen with its own icon. No app-store install required.
