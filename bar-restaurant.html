What you get

Everything the counter, the store and the owner need.

Open tabs and fast sales

Open a bill, add rounds through the night, split or settle at the end. No lost paper tickets, no arguments over who ordered what.

Stock that adds up

Every sale draws down stock. Restock, count and see what is running low before the shelf is empty.

Reports you can print

Daily and period reports as clean PDFs, plus 80mm slips for the printer at your counter.

Staff roles and logins

Each person signs in with their own account. You decide who can sell, who can see money and who can change prices.

Installs like an app

No app store. Open the link, tap install, and it sits on the phone home screen next to everything else.

Backed up for you

Your data is saved on a schedule and kept on a secure server, so a lost phone does not mean lost records.

Takeaway and delivery

Your own ordering page. No commission to anyone.

Customers open your link, browse drinks, food and sides, and place an order. They follow it until it arrives and get alerts at each step.

How an order flows

From phone to doorstep in five steps.

1
Customer orders

Picks items, drops a delivery pin on the map, sends the order.

2
Fee is agreed

Staff quote the transport fee. Both sides can offer and counter, then confirm.

3
Bar prepares

Order is confirmed, slip is printed, items leave stock.

4
Rider delivers

Rider opens a private link. The customer tracks the trip live.

5
Payment recorded

Mark it paid and it lands in your sales and reports, delivery fee included.
