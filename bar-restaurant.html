How an order flows

From phone to doorstep in five steps.

1

Customer orders. Picks items, drops a delivery pin on the map, sends the order.

2

Fee is agreed. Staff quote the transport fee. Both sides can offer and counter, then confirm.

3

Bar prepares. Order is confirmed, slip is printed, items leave stock.

4

Rider delivers. Rider opens a private link. The customer tracks the trip live.

5

Payment recorded. Mark it paid and it lands in your sales and reports, delivery fee included.
