Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Osubula Talks Solutions ("we") provides a school fee and results management system for schools in Uganda. This policy explains what we do with data, including when a school connects its Google Drive for backups. We act in line with Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019.

Who is responsible

Each school is the controller of its own students', parents' and staff data. We process that data on the school's behalf to run the system. Contact: osubuladan@gmail.com.

Data we process

Student, parent and staff records, fee and payment records, attendance and results, entered by the school. Sign-in details for system users.

Google Drive backups

If a school administrator chooses to connect Google Drive, we ask for two things only: the Google account email address (to show which account is connected) and the "drive.file" permission, which lets the app see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself. We cannot see, open or change any other file in the school's Drive.

The app creates a folder in the school's own Drive and saves encrypted backup files there. The backups are encrypted before they leave our servers; they can only be read with the system's keys. We keep the connection token in encrypted form and use it only to save and tidy up backup files. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting

A school administrator can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the Backups page. This revokes our access and stops all new backups. Existing backup files stay in the school's Drive; the school can delete them there.

Security and retention

Data is stored in secured databases and encrypted backups. We keep data for as long as the school uses the system and as the school's own retention rules require, and we delete or return it when the school asks.

Your rights

You may ask the school, or us, to access, correct or delete your personal data. Write to osubuladan@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
